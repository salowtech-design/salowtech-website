## The Dual Search Era: Google + AI Answer Engines

Users no longer just browse blue links; they ask direct questions to Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Google AI Overviews. To be cited as the authoritative source, your content must provide direct, unambiguous definitions and structured data.

## Topical Authority Clustering

Rather than publishing standalone disconnected blog posts, build interconnected content pillars that thoroughly answer every sub-question within your core industry domain.
