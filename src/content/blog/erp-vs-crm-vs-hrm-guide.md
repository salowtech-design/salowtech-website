## Demystifying the Three Enterprise Pillars

As businesses scale beyond single-location operations, software requirements bifurcate into customer relationships, physical operations, and human payroll. Choosing which system to build first depends on your primary bottleneck.

## When to Prioritize CRM First

If your biggest operational challenge is losing prospective leads, delayed sales follow-ups, or lack of pipeline forecasting, invest first in a custom CRM. It delivers the fastest direct cashflow return on investment.

## When ERP Becomes Non-Negotiable

If stock discrepancies occur across warehouses, purchase orders do not reconcile with accounts payable, or manufacturing shop floors face material shortages, an ERP system is the critical foundation.
