## Why ERP Data is High-Target

Financial ledgers, customer pricing agreements, and supplier bank records represent the crown jewels of enterprise operations. Securing this data requires defense in depth across network, application, and database tiers.

## Cryptographic Audit Trails

Every ledger debit, credit, inventory write-off, or purchase order modification must be permanently written to an append-only log with cryptographic hashing to guarantee tamper evidence.
