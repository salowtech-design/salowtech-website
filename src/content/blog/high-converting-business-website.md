## Performance is the First Feature

Every additional second of page load latency reduces conversion rates by up to 20%. Modern web engineering achieves sub-second Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) by minimizing third-party script bloat, pre-rendering critical CSS, and serving responsive modern image formats.

## Editorial Authority vs Generic Tech Clutter

High-ticket buyers judge your engineering capability by the polish and restraint of your digital storefront. Calm neutrals, warm serif typography, and balanced white space convey institutional credibility.
