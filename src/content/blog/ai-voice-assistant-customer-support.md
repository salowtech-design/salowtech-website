## The Death of Robotic IVR Menus

Nobody enjoys listening to "Press 1 for Sales, Press 2 for Support". Modern enterprise voice assistants allow callers to speak naturally in everyday phrasing, comprehending intent and nuance instantly.

## Sub-400ms Response Latency

To feel like a natural conversation, an AI voice system must respond in under 450 milliseconds. By streaming audio tokens over WebSockets, the assistant begins articulating before the entire LLM generation completes.
