## Why Traditional CRM Implementations Fail

Most off-the-shelf CRMs fail not because of missing features, but because of excessive complexity. Sales representatives spend more time filling out tedious mandatory form fields than speaking with qualified buyers. When data entry feels like homework, data becomes stale and pipeline visibility evaporates.

## Automating the Sub-5-Minute Lead Window

Research demonstrates that contacting an inbound lead within five minutes yields an exponential lift in qualification rates compared to waiting 30 minutes. Custom CRM engineering triggers instant automated WhatsApp qualification sequences and routes high-intent prospects straight to available account executives.

## Unified Omnichannel Communication Hub

Bring WhatsApp conversations, VoIP phone recordings, email threads, and client contracts into one unified timeline. Every rep on your squad has complete contextual memory before dialling a client.
