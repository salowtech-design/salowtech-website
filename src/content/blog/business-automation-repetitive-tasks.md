## Identify the Repetition Tax

If any member of your team performs the exact same sequence of copy-paste keystrokes more than three times a week, that task is an immediate candidate for programmatic automation.

## The Power of Event-Driven Microservices

Rather than relying on fragile manual triggers, modern businesses configure webhook event buses. When a contract is e-signed, a CRM record updates, an invoice generates, and onboarding credentials disburse automatically.
