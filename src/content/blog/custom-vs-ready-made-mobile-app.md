## The False Economy of Template Apps

Ready-made templates often seem attractive for rapid validation. However, as soon as a business needs customized biometric verification, proprietary offline ledgers, or unique checkout flows, templates require costly workarounds and often break.

## Offline-First Architecture

For logistics, field sales, and warehouse apps, network drops are a daily reality. Bespoke apps store state locally and queue bi-directional sync batches seamlessly without interrupting the user.
