## The Frustration of Channel Fragmentation

In typical organizations, customer conversations live in separate silos: sales talks on WhatsApp, support replies over email, and account managers make phone calls without logging notes. This lack of shared context leads to repeated questions and slow resolutions.

## Real-Time Bi-Directional Event Routing

When a customer contacts your team on any touchpoint, the omnichannel hub identifies their account, surfaces past invoices, open tickets, and recent delivery updates in under 200 milliseconds.
