Frequently Asked Questions

Direct, factual answers to key questions about Salowtech engineering, commercial terms, and sovereign technology.

Salowtech engineers full-stack custom enterprise software: bespoke ERP systems, custom CRM platforms, native and cross-platform mobile apps (iOS & Android), high-speed web applications, statutory Indian HRM and payroll portals, conversational AI voice agents, and technical SEO growth engineering.

Under the sovereign software doctrine, clients receive 100% intellectual property and full source code ownership upon milestone delivery. There are zero perpetual per-seat license fees, zero mandatory subscription lock-ins, and full freedom to deploy on private cloud or on-premise servers.

Pricing is fixed-scope and milestone-based, determined by exact architectural modules and workflow complexity. After an initial confidential discovery consultation, Salowtech delivers a transparent sprint-based cost roadmap within 24 hours, ensuring zero hidden fees or unplanned recurring costs.

Production MVPs and focused enterprise modules typically launch in 4 to 8 weeks. Comprehensive multi-plant ERPs or omnichannel platforms take 8 to 14 weeks, delivered in transparent two-week agile sprints with working software demos after each milestone.

A CRM manages customer pipelines, lead conversions, and sales communications. An HRM automates employee records, biometric attendance, and statutory payroll filings. An ERP is the central nervous system synchronizing inventory, manufacturing, procurement, accounting, and multi-warehouse operations.

Salowtech AI voice agents handle inbound telephony, website chat, and WhatsApp inquiries with sub-second response latency. Grounded in your private product knowledge base via Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), they qualify leads, book appointments, and resolve support tickets 24/7.

Salowtech builds on battle-tested modern stacks: React, Next.js, and TypeScript on frontend; Node.js, Python, and Go on backend; PostgreSQL, MySQL, and Redis for data; Flutter, Swift, and Kotlin for mobile; and Supabase, AWS, and Cloudflare for infrastructure.
