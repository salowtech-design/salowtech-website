# How to Automate Indian Statutory Payroll (PF, ESIC, PT, TDS) Without Spreadsheet Errors

### Executive Summary (Direct Answer)
Automating Indian statutory payroll requires synchronizing biometric punch registers with dynamic gross-to-net calculation engines that strictly enforce the Employees' Provident Funds Act, 1952, ESIC contribution rules, state-specific Professional Tax (PT) slabs, and dual-regime TDS withholding. Salowtech custom HRM software automates this entire pipeline into a single-click monthly run, generating statutory ECR upload files and bank NEFT disbursement batches with zero human spreadsheet error.

### Key Statutory Deductions Automated in Modern HRMS
1. **Employees' Provident Fund (EPF)**: Automatically calculates 12% employee contribution on eligible wage ceiling (₹15,000) and splits employer's 12% into EPF (3.67%) and EPS (8.33%), producing pre-formatted Electronic Challan cum Return (ECR) text files for one-click portal filing.
2. **Employees' State Insurance (ESIC)**: Monitors gross wage thresholds (₹21,000 monthly ceiling) and automatically computes 0.75% employee contribution and 3.25% employer share.
3. **Professional Tax (PT)**: Dynamically applies state-specific monthly slabs (e.g. Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, West Bengal, Tamil Nadu) including February adjustment cycles.
4. **Income Tax TDS (Section 192)**: Compares Old Tax Regime (with 80C, 80D, HRA deductions from Form 12BB) against the New Concessional Tax Regime (Section 115BAC), automatically withholding exact monthly tax installments.

### End-to-End Payroll Workflow: Biometric Punch to Bank Transfer
1. **Biometric Listener Capture**: Daily punch logs from eSSL/ZKTeco devices or GPS geofenced mobile clock-ins automatically sync into attendance tables.
2. **Leave & Shift Reconciliation**: Automated sandwich-leave rules, overtime approvals, and unpaid leaves reconcile against working days.
3. **One-Click Calculation**: The system computes net salary, allowances, and statutory contributions in seconds.
4. **Direct Bank File Export**: Formats batch payment text/Excel files for HDFC, ICICI, SBI, and Axis Bank for instant bulk salary disbursement without manual voucher entry.
5. **Employee Self-Service (ESS)**: Password-protected PDF payslips are automatically published to employee mobile portals.
